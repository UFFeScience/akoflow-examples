# Data attribution

**INPE DETER / TerraBrasilis.** The alert records originate from the Brazilian
National Institute for Space Research (INPE), General Coordination of Earth
Sciences. The [layer metadata](https://terrabrasilis.dpi.inpe.br/geonetwork/srv/por/csw?service=CSW&version=2.0.2&request=GetRecordById&elementSetName=full&outputSchema=csw:IsoRecord&id=f2153c4a-915b-48a6-8658-963bdce7366c)
identifies the layer as licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
The snapshot selects records by observation month, retains their properties and
geometries, and normalizes JSON serialization before compression. Derived
alert tables and maps are shared under the same license.

**IBGE.** The maps use Brazil's 2024 state boundaries from the [IBGE GeoFTP
distribution](https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/malhas_territoriais/malhas_municipais/municipio_2024/Brasil/).
The boundary source and its SHA-256 are recorded in
[evidence/ibge-boundary-source.json](evidence/ibge-boundary-source.json).

Cite the TerraBrasilis platform as [Assis et al., 2019](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijgi8110513).
